# NNNN. Short title of the decision

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

One to three short paragraphs: the context, what was decided, and why. An ADR records that a
decision was made and the reason, so a later reader does not "fix" something deliberate.

Write one only when the decision is hard to reverse, would surprise a reader without context,
and came from a real trade-off. Number it with the next free number; never renumber.

## Consequences

Optional. Only the non-obvious effects downstream.

## Considered options

Optional. Only alternatives whose rejection is worth remembering.
